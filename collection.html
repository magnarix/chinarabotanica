Marine botanicals

MARIS is built on a simple idea: the plants that survive the sea know something about resilience. Here is the botanical at its centre, and the thinking behind the oil.

One marine botanical Research-led

Where it began

From the laboratory
to the bottle

MARIS is inspired by doctoral research into the remarkable chemistry of brown algae. The pigment at the centre of that work — fucoxanthin — is the very marine ingredient in MARIS today.

That research never became a claim. It became a discipline — a way of choosing ingredients carefully, and of leaving out anything that does not belong.
