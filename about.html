Our Story

Skincare that began
as a question.

Chinara Botanica did not begin with a business plan, or a gap in the market. It began with a fascination — for the plants of the sea, and the quiet chemistry they use to survive. In time, that fascination became a way of thinking about skincare: fewer ingredients, chosen with intention, and worn as a daily ritual rather than a quick fix.

We think of the ocean as the most patient chemist there is — and of the plants that endure its extremes as quietly extraordinary.

Everything we make begins there: in curiosity about the sea, and in the belief that caring for your skin should feel like a ritual, not a routine.

Where It Began

A curiosity that
never quite left

My path to skincare ran through a laboratory. For several years, my doctoral research in pharmaceutical science was spent in the company of brown algae — the unshowy seaweeds that hold fast to rocks where the tide is roughest. I was drawn to them for a simple reason: they endure conditions that would undo most plants, and they do it with a kind of quiet resilience.

What held me was how they protect themselves. Sun, salt, the constant pull of the sea — and yet they thrive, in part through the antioxidants they make for themselves. Among these is a golden pigment called fucoxanthin: the colour of late-afternoon light, held inside a plant. It seemed genuinely lovely to me that something so humble could be so quietly clever.

That chapter came to an end, as such things do. The curiosity did not. Long after the work was finished, I kept returning to those marine molecules — not as data, but as something I wanted to bring into ordinary life. It felt almost natural that they should become skincare: something you return to each day, without ceremony.

Chosen,
never added.

We begin by leaving things out. A formulation has to earn its shortness — every ingredient must justify its place, and anything included only to lengthen a label stays on the bench.

Quality, to us, is not a longer list of actives. It is the right ingredients, well sourced and well understood. We would rather do a few things beautifully than many things adequately.

The sea,
respected.

Our defining ingredients come from the ocean, led by brown algae. We build around what the sea has already perfected, rather than trying to improve on it.

That respect extends to how we source. The sea is not an endless shelf — we take carefully, and only what we need, so that what we make sits lightly on the world it comes from.

A ritual,
not a trend.

We are not interested in the next thing. Skincare, at its best, is slow — an elegant, simple gesture, repeated until it quietly becomes part of you.

So we design for that: for the pleasure of a few drops, warmed and pressed into the skin, morning or night. A ritual to keep, rather than a trend to chase.

Today

One oil, and what
comes after it

Today, that curiosity lives in a single bottle. MARIS, our marine facial oil, is the first formulation in the Marine Ritual Collection — the beginning of a small, considered family of products, each built around the botanicals of the sea.

There is no hurry to grow. Future formulations will arrive slowly, only when they have something to add — and always with the same philosophy: marine-led, simply made, and meant to be lived with.

Chinara Botanica is less a company
than a continuation —
of curiosity, and of care.

The work ended years ago. The wonder never did. Everything we make is a way of keeping that conversation with the sea alive — and of passing a little of it on to you.
