About

We are a
formulation house.

We create botanical compositions for daily rituals — not products for shelves. Each composition is designed to accompany a moment in your day, turning the ordinary into something felt.

We believe that how you begin your day, how you sustain your afternoon, and how you close your evening — these are not small things.

They are the structure of a life.

Compositions,
not blends.

A blend combines. A composition arranges. We approach each ritual the way a musician approaches a piece — choosing notes not for what they do alone, but for how they resonate together.

Nothing is added for volume. Nothing is there by accident. Every botanical earns its place through character and harmony.

Rhythm,
not intensity.

We don't design for peak performance. We design for the texture of a day — its rhythms, its transitions, its quiet thresholds.

Morning clarity. Afternoon grounding. Evening release. Our compositions move with you, not against you. They don't push. They accompany.

Sensation,
not claims.

We will never tell you what a botanical does to your body. We will tell you how it feels in the cup — the warmth, the aroma, the way it changes the air around you.

Our promise is sensory, not clinical. We trust you to notice what you notice.

The Process

How a composition is made

01

Source

We work directly with small-batch growers across Japan, India, and the Mediterranean. People who tend their plants with the same patience we bring to our compositions. Every botanical is evaluated for provenance and character, not just quality.

02

Listen

Before we compose, we steep each ingredient alone. We learn its voice — its weight, its warmth, its arc from first sip to finish. Only when we understand a botanical on its own terms do we consider how it might speak alongside another.

03

Compose

A single ritual may go through dozens of iterations. We adjust ratios by fractions. We taste in silence. We wait days between sessions so our palate returns fresh. The composition is complete when nothing can be added and nothing can be removed.

04

Name

Each composition is named for its moment, not its ingredients. Refresh. Daily. Study. Post-Meal. Evening. The name tells you when. The cup tells you everything else.

What Guides Us

Intention

Every decision — from sourcing to packaging — is made deliberately. We choose the slow path because it leads to better outcomes.

Restraint

We believe in the power of less. Fewer ingredients, composed more carefully. Fewer products, made more thoughtfully.

Presence

Our rituals are designed to bring you back to the moment. Not to optimize or enhance — simply to be here, now, fully.

The best ritual is the one you return to.

We don't ask you to change your day.
We ask you to notice it.
