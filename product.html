The Study Ritual

Calm, sustained clarity

Study Ritual is a roasted botanical composition that supports focus without stimulation. No spike, no crash — just a steady, grounded presence that stays with you through the afternoon.

Where coffee pushes, this composition settles. It creates the conditions for deep work by quieting everything that isn't essential.

What to expect

The Composition

Roasted roots. Fresh herbs.
Nothing more.

Base

Roasted Green Tea

Japanese Hojicha — slow-roasted over charcoal. Deep, warm, earthy. The foundation that grounds every cup.

Heart

Ginkgo Leaf

Ancient, considered, quietly powerful. A botanical companion to clear thinking — used for centuries, never hurried.

Accent

Rosemary

A fresh, herbal lift. Just enough brightness to keep the composition from turning inward. The element that keeps you present.

Tasting Notes

Roasted Root Warm, deep, charcoal-kissed
Fresh Herb Clean, aromatic, grounding
Gentle Spice A whisper of warmth at the finish

Preparation

The brewing ritual

01

Heat

Bring water to 85°C (185°F). Not boiling — just below. The roasted leaves prefer patience.

02

Measure

One heaped teaspoon per cup. Place into your vessel of choice — a pot, a gaiwan, whatever feels right.

03

Steep

3–4 minutes for the first infusion. The roasted base reveals itself first. Steep again for the herbal subtleties.

04

Begin

Let the aroma ground you before the first sip. This is the transition — from scattered to settled.
