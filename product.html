The Oil

One oil, composed with care

MARIS is a single, waterless facial oil rather than a routine of many. Marine-derived fucoxanthin sits at its centre, carried by cold-pressed squalane, jojoba, and rosehip seed oil — five ingredients, chosen for how lightly they wear.

The result is an oil that feels almost dry to the touch — present, but never heavy. It sinks in quickly and leaves a soft, natural radiance.

The Composition

Five ingredients.
Nothing for show.

The Marine Active

Fucoxanthin

A marine-derived carotenoid from brown algae. It gives MARIS its natural golden colour, and it is the ingredient that first drew our curiosity to the sea.

The Base Oils

Squalane & Jojoba

Plant-derived squalane and jojoba seed oil give MARIS its weightless, dry-touch feel — close to skin's own, so it wears like almost nothing.

The Botanical Finish

Rosehip & Vitamin E

Cold-pressed rosehip seed oil, finished with a little tocopherol (Vitamin E) to help keep the delicate oils fresh. No added fragrance, ever.

How to Use

The daily ritual

01

Cleanse

Begin with clean skin, lightly damp. MARIS is the final step — apply it after any water-based care.

02

Draw

Three to four drops from the dropper into a clean palm. A little is enough for the whole face.

03

Warm

Press palms together for a moment. Warmth thins the oil so it glides easily over the skin.

04

Press

Press gently over the face and throat — no rubbing, letting the skin take it in. Morning or night, every day.
