About the Oil
MARIS is a lightweight, waterless (anhydrous) marine facial oil. It's formulated around marine-derived fucoxanthin — a golden carotenoid from brown algae — carried by squalane, jojoba seed oil, and rosehip seed oil, and finished with a little Vitamin E. One considered oil, made for daily use.
The colour comes from marine carotenoids, including fucoxanthin. Nothing is added to tint it. Because it's a botanical oil, the exact shade may vary a little from batch to batch — a sign of the ingredients, not a fault.
MARIS is a facial oil — the nourishing final step of a routine. It's simple enough to wear on its own, or pressed over a water-based product. It seals in what comes before it.

Using MARIS
After cleansing, warm three to four drops between your palms and press gently over the face and throat — no rubbing. Use it as the last step of your routine.
